Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Welcome to Money Tracker. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you use our application (developed by Nikhil Gupta).

1. Information We Collect

When you use Money Tracker, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and authentication details obtained securely through Firebase Authentication (including Google Sign-In).
  • Financial Data: Transaction records, descriptions, amounts, categories, and custom collections that you manually input into the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is strictly used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and secure your personal finance dashboard.
  • Sync your transaction records securely with our backend database hosted on Render and MongoDB Atlas.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust security measures to protect your financial records. Your data is stored securely in our database. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties.

4. Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can permanently delete your account and all associated financial records at any time directly from the app's Settings menu ("Delete Account"), which instantly removes your data from our servers.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly within this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out to the developer, Nikhil Gupta, via our official portfolio or support channels.
